Privacy Policy

David George Ventures · Effective October 14, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play mobile games published by David George Ventures ("we", "us"), including SpinnerIdle, and how it is used. If you have any question about it, write to mathisawesome866@gmail.com.

What we collect

Our games do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and you do not need an account to play. Through the analytics services listed below, the game collects:

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system and version, language, country (derived from your IP address), app version, and a randomly generated install identifier.
  • Advertising identifiers: your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS), only where you have allowed it.
  • Gameplay events: sessions and their length, levels started, won or lost, upgrades, and similar in-game actions.
  • Install attribution: how you found the game (for example, which store page or campaign led to the install).

Your progress is saved on your device. It is not uploaded to us.

Why we use it

  • To understand how the game is played and improve its balance, difficulty and stability.
  • To measure where installs come from and how well a game performs.
  • To fix crashes and technical problems.

We do not sell your personal information. Our games currently show no advertising.

Partners who process this information

The game uses the TinySauce SDK by Voodoo, which includes the services below. Each processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Voodoo (game publishing and analytics): voodoo.io/privacy
  • GameAnalytics (gameplay analytics): gameanalytics.com/privacy
  • Adjust (install attribution): adjust.com/terms/privacy-policy
  • Meta / Facebook SDK (app events and install measurement): facebook.com/privacy/policy

Your choices and consent

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before analytics or advertising tracking starts. You can change your choice at any time from the game's privacy settings.
  • On iOS, the game asks for permission before it accesses the IDFA, and you can change this in your device settings.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information held about you, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, email mathisawesome866@gmail.com. Please include the game's name and, if you can, your device's advertising ID so the request can be matched. We will answer within 30 days and pass deletion requests on to the partners above.

Retention

Analytics data is kept only as long as it is needed for the purposes above, and in any case according to each partner's retention policy. Data you ask us to delete is deleted.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and it will be deleted.

Security and transfers

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Our partners may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, with safeguards required by applicable law.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
